Common Questions

  • How do I cancel my PRO subscription?
    Go to your device's App Store settings → Subscriptions → Expense Manager PRO → Cancel.
  • How do I delete my account?
    Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be removed within 30 days.
  • My transactions aren't syncing.
    Check your internet connection. The app works offline and syncs automatically when connectivity is restored.
  • I lost access to my account.
    Sign in again using the same Google or Apple account you used to register.
