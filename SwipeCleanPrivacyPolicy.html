1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device. SwipeClean reads them locally to show them in the swipe deck and to delete them (with your confirmation) through the operating system.
  • We do not run our own servers, accounts, or cloud backup of your gallery.
  • We use a small number of third-party SDKs for crash reporting (Sentry), advertising (Google AdMob), and in-app purchases (RevenueCat). They process limited technical data as described below.
  • You can stop data processing at any time by uninstalling the App or revoking permissions in your system settings.

2. Data we access and process

2.1 Photos and videos (on-device only)

SwipeClean requests read access to your photo library so it can display your media inside the swipe interface. When you confirm deletion, the App asks the operating system (iOS / Android) to remove the selected items from your device gallery using the system's native deletion dialog. We do not upload, copy, sync, or transmit your photos or videos to any server. All processing happens locally on your device.

2.2 App settings and local state

The App stores the following information locally on your device using SharedPreferences and Hive:

  • Your preferences (reminder time, frequency, session mode, etc.).
  • A list of photo identifiers you have already reviewed, so the same photos do not reappear. These identifiers are local references to items in your gallery; they are not the photos themselves.
  • The internal "trash" queue of items pending deletion (also identifiers only).

This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or use the in-app reset option.

2.3 Local notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your operating system. We do not send push notifications from a server and we do not collect notification engagement data.

2.4 Diagnostic and technical data

If the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, limited diagnostic data is sent to our crash-reporting provider (Sentry, see Section 3) so we can fix the bug. This typically includes:

  • Crash stack trace and error type.
  • App version, build number, locale.
  • Device model, operating system version.
  • Non-identifying breadcrumbs (e.g. "user opened settings screen").

We do not deliberately attach your name, email, photos, photo content, precise location, or contacts to crash reports.

3. Third-party services

The following independent providers may process limited data on our behalf or directly under their own privacy policies. We recommend you review their policies.

3.1 Sentry (crash and error reporting)

  • Purpose: capture crashes and runtime errors to improve stability.
  • Data: stack traces, app version, device model, OS version, locale, breadcrumbs.
  • Policy: sentry.io/privacy

3.2 Google AdMob (advertising)

  • Purpose: showing advertising inside the App, including measurement and limited personalization where allowed.
  • Data typically processed by Google: advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse device data, interaction with ads.
  • On iOS, AdMob only receives the IDFA if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, ads are served in a non-personalized mode.
  • Policies: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & data

3.3 RevenueCat (in-app purchases and subscriptions)

  • Purpose: manage in-app purchases and subscription entitlements, sync purchase state across reinstalls.
  • Data: an anonymous RevenueCat user ID generated on first launch, purchase receipts validated with Apple App Store / Google Play, basic device and platform metadata.
  • Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

3.4 Apple App Store and Google Play

When you install, update, or purchase the App, Apple or Google process data under their own privacy policies. We receive aggregate, anonymized installation and purchase metrics through their dashboards. We never receive your payment card or full account details.

4. Device permissions

  • Photo library (iOS NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription / Android READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): required to display and delete items from your gallery.
  • Notifications (iOS NSUserNotificationsUsageDescription / Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS): required to deliver local reminders you have scheduled.
  • Exact alarms / boot completed (Android SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): required so reminders fire on the time you chose, including after a device restart.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): optional. Used only to allow Google AdMob to access the IDFA for personalized ads. Declining keeps ads in non-personalized mode.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device Settings.

5. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Providing the core App functionality (gallery access, reminders, trash, purchases) — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Crash and error reporting — legitimate interest in keeping the App stable and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Advertising (non-personalized) — legitimate interest in monetizing the free version (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Advertising (personalized) and tracking via IDFA / Advertising ID — consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), obtained through the system consent dialog where required.
  • Purchases and subscription management — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Data retention

  • On-device data (settings, reviewed-photo IDs, trash queue) is kept until you reset it from the App or uninstall the App.
  • Crash reports in Sentry are kept according to Sentry's default retention (typically up to 90 days) and then deleted.
  • AdMob and RevenueCat retain technical data as described in their own policies.

7. Children's privacy

SwipeClean is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that requires our attention, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EEA, UK, California, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion.
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. ATT on iOS).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we don't operate user accounts and don't hold your photos, most deletion requests are satisfied by simply uninstalling the App and revoking ATT consent. For crash data tied to your device, contact us at {{EMAIL}} and we will work with Sentry to delete it where feasible.

9. Security

Because your photos and personal preferences stay on your device, they benefit from the security model of your operating system (sandboxing, device encryption, screen lock). Data transmitted to third-party services (Sentry, AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. International data transfers

Our third-party processors (Sentry, Google, RevenueCat, Apple) may store and process data outside your country of residence, including in the United States. They rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or other valid transfer mechanisms under GDPR / UK GDPR.
