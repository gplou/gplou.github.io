Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

GPM ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Expense Manager mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

By using Expense Manager, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address, provided via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In.

1.2 Financial Data

To provide expense-tracking functionality, we store:

  • Transactions you create (amounts, descriptions, categories, dates).
  • Custom categories you define.
  • Recurring transaction rules you configure.

1.3 Subscription Information

If you subscribe to Expense Manager PRO, we store your purchase history and subscription status. Payment details are processed exclusively by Apple (App Store). We never have access to your credit card or payment method information.

1.4 Media and Voice Data

When you use AI-powered transaction entry, we may temporarily process photos of receipts and audio recordings for voice-based entry. This media is sent to our server-side functions for parsing only and is not stored permanently — it is discarded after the transaction data has been extracted.

1.5 Analytics Data

We use PostHog for product analytics to understand how features are used and to improve the App. Analytics data is pseudonymous and does not include your financial transactions.

1.6 Local Preferences

App preferences (theme, language, display settings) are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's core expense-tracking features.
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • Process AI-assisted transaction entry (voice and image) via Supabase Edge Functions powered by Gemini AI (Google).
  • Power the AI chat assistant for financial insights based on your transaction data.
  • Manage your PRO subscription status.
  • Measure and improve product features via anonymised analytics (PostHog).
  • Show relevant ads in the free tier (Google AdMob).

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted backend platform. Supabase implements industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.
  • Row-level security so you can only access your own data.
  • Secure authentication via OAuth providers (Google, Apple).

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Backend Supabase — database, authentication, and serverless functions.
  • Auth Google Sign-In
  • Auth Apple Sign-In
  • AI Gemini AI (Google) — voice/image parsing and chat, accessed via Supabase Edge Functions.
  • Subscriptions RevenueCat — in-app purchase management.
  • Ads Google AdMob — advertising in the free tier.
  • Analytics PostHog — product analytics.

5. Advertising and App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

The free tier of Expense Manager shows ads via Google AdMob. On iOS, the App may request permission to use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You can grant or deny this permission; denying it does not affect the core functionality of the App.

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes beyond what AdMob requires with your consent.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and transaction data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data from our servers within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Expense Manager is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

9. International Users

The App is available in Spanish, English, French, and German. Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the App, you consent to such transfer and processing.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy within the App and updating the "Last updated" date above.
